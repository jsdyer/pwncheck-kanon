What happens when you check

  1. 1 / IN YOUR BROWSER

    Make a fingerprint

    Your browser turns the exact password into a 40-character SHA-1 hash. This is for lookup, not a recommendation for storing passwords.

  2. 2 / AT THE PROVIDER

    Ask for a range

    Only the first five characters go to Pwned Passwords. It returns matching hash endings, plus padding to reduce response-size clues.

  3. 3 / BACK IN THIS TAB

    Compare the rest

    Your browser checks the remaining 35 characters against that range. A match means this password is in the provider’s exposed-password dataset.

Know the limits

Exposure is not strength.

A missing match is not a safety certificate. This checks a password value, not your email address, an account, or every breach that has ever happened. Counts describe occurrences in the dataset, not affected people or separate breaches.

Privacy by design

No history to clear later.

This tool uses no analytics, remote fonts, browser storage or check history. Clear everything removes the visible input and result. Your browser, extensions and network still matter; the prefix request is not perfect anonymity.

Source, privacy & further reading +

Results come from Have I Been Pwned · Pwned Passwords. The dataset changes over time; this tool does not keep previous checks or identify a particular leak.
